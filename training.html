01 / CURRICULUM

The Training Pathway

Four phases, run in fixed order. Prior experience shortens nothing — the standard doesn't flex.

01
Phase 01 · 2 Weeks

Orientation & Ethics

Company standards, applicable security law, professional conduct, and the reporting discipline that makes an incident log useful.

02
Phase 02 · 3 Weeks

Physical & Tactical Conditioning

Fitness benchmarks, restraint technique, and patrol movement drilled until they don't require thinking about.

03
Phase 03 · 3 Weeks

Threat Recognition & Response

Reading behavior before it escalates, de-escalation, first aid/CPR, and staged emergency scenarios under evaluation.

04
Phase 04 · 2 Weeks

Live Deployment & Certification

A supervised live posting, followed by formal evaluation and instructor sign-off on actual fieldwork.

02 / STANDARDS

What Training Instills

The principles that shape every module, drill, and officer we certify.

Training Vision

  • To be recognized for producing India's most disciplined, field-ready security officers.
  • Setting benchmark standards in protective training, not just protective services.

Training Mission

  • Certify every officer against one uniform standard, regardless of prior experience.
  • Disciplined execution through continuous, scenario-based tactical training.

What We Instill

  • Integrity — Absolute honesty and transparency in every assignment.
  • Vigilance — Continuous alertness to recognize threats before they arise.
  • Excellence — Superior standards in physical and tactical readiness.
03 / SPECIALIZATION

Tracks After Certification

Officers are routed into a track matched to the posting they'll actually hold.

T·01

Armed Response Unit

Firearms handling, threat neutralization protocol, live-fire qualification under range supervision.

T·02

Executive Protection

Close protection detail, advance route planning, low-profile posture in civilian settings.

T·03

Corporate & Facility Security

Access control, patrol logging, incident escalation coordinated with facility management.

T·04

Event & Crowd Management

Crowd flow, VIP movement, emergency egress planning for high-density events.

T·05

Fire & Emergency Response

Evacuation drills, fire suppression equipment, first-responder coordination.

T·06

Surveillance & Access Control

CCTV monitoring, visitor screening procedure, timed alarm response.
